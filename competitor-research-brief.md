## 1. Winning patterns (what the best sites all do)

1. **Free-estimate CTA + click-to-call on every screen.** Arbor Elite, Bigfoot, Wood Goat, and Mike's repeat the phone number and "free estimate" CTA in the hero, mid-page, and footer. Nobody makes you hunt for how to contact them.
2. **Before/after photo galleries as the #1 trust builder.** Bigfoot's 60+ before/after pairs and Wood Goat's action shots do the selling. This work is visual; photos beat adjectives.
3. **Named local owner with photo and story.** Bigfoot (Cody Gunter, grandfather's logging business), Wood Goat (Addison Corley), Mike's (owner photo), Arbor Elite (Bill & Laura). Family-owned/local beats faceless every time.
4. **Trust-badge checklist.** Mike's and Arbor Elite both run an explicit checklist: Licensed & Insured, Free Estimates, Satisfaction Guaranteed, years in business, 24-hr emergency. Scannable in 5 seconds.
5. **FAQ / "what to expect" section.** Wood Goat's FAQ (what gets removed, how long, debris handling) and RLTW's honest-expectations copy (upfront about possible ruts) answer the questions every landowner asks before calling.

## 2. Gaps we can exploit

1. **Nobody leads with veteran-owned.** Only Mike's offers a "Discount for Veterans" as a bullet; no competitor brands itself veteran-owned & operated. ThorSon's can own this outright — and it doubles as an edge for municipal/government B2B work.
2. **Weak Camden-local presence.** Competitors cluster in Columbia/Lexington. Mike's is the only Camden tree service with a real site (and it's dated); Otis Lee (Elgin) and Graves (Camden) have thin/weak sites. "Camden's land services company" is open lane.
3. **Zero pricing transparency.** No one publishes prices or even pricing factors. An "what affects your estimate" explainer (stump diameter/count/access/cleanup; acreage/vegetation density for mulching) would be unique in the market — without committing to numbers.
4. **Commercial/B2B is underserved.** Only Land Clearing Services Inc and Bigfoot speak to commercial clients; nobody has a dedicated contractors / property managers / HOAs / developers section. ThorSon's stated B2B strategy has a clear opening.
5. **Estimate-request forms are rare and shallow.** Only Wood Goat has a real intake form ("Tell Us About Your Land"). Most sites are phone-only. A good form that lets people describe the job (and attach photos) captures leads competitors lose after hours.

## 3. Specific recommendations for ThorSon's Google Sites site

**Home**
- Add a trust-badge strip directly under the hero: "Veteran-Owned & Operated · Free Estimates · Serving Camden & the Midlands" (+ "Licensed & Insured" only if true — verify before publishing).
- Add a before/after photo section as soon as first jobs are done — even 3–4 pairs. This is the single highest-ROI addition; benchmark is Bigfoot's gallery.
- Make sure the click-to-call button is prominent and thumb-friendly on mobile (most "stump grinding near me" searches happen on phones).

**Services**
- Add "What affects your estimate" explainer under each service: stump grinding → stump diameter, number of stumps, access, cleanup/haul-off options; forestry mulching → acreage, vegetation density, terrain; brush hogging → acreage, overgrowth height, terrain. No competitor does this.
- Add a short FAQ per service modeled on Wood Goat: "How deep do you grind?", "What happens to the mulch/chips?", "Do I need to be home?", "How long does a typical job take?"

**About Us**
- Keep the veteran-owned & operated lead and the Thorson name story; add real photos of Chris and his father plus the equipment (Bobcat 883, mulcher, trucks/trailers). Named owners + real equipment photos beat every competitor's stock imagery. Commercial clients especially care about equipment — Land Clearing Services Inc lists theirs.

**Contact**
- Embed a Google Form estimate request capturing: name, phone, property address, service needed, job description, and photo upload. Frame it as Wood Goat does — 3 steps: "Tell us about your land → Get your free estimate → Watch it transform."
- Keep the town service-area list; add Arbor Elite's closer: "Don't see your town? Call anyway — we travel for the right job."
- Keep "estimate" (never "quote") throughout — already done.

### Vendor registration / procurement notes
- **City of Sumter** — formal Procurement page with Vendor Registration (sumtersc.gov). Procurement contact: David Ferrell, Asset and Operations Manager, sumtersc.gov/purchasing.
- **City of Columbia** — uses the **eBidcolumbiasc** online vendor system; procurements over $25,000 go through Procurement & Contracts.
- **Richland County** — formal Vendor Registration & Qualification via the Office of Procurement & Contracting (needs insurance cert, W-9, SC SOS certificate, LLR registration where applicable).
- **Lexington County** — Bidder/Vendor Application via Procurement Services, (803) 785-8107; bids posted on the ionwave.net portal.
- **Kershaw County** — Bidder/Vendor Application available on kershaw.sc.gov.
- City of Camden, Sumter County, and City of Columbia forestry/street divisions: no dedicated vendor-registration page surfaced — main offices are the entry point.

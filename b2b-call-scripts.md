# ThorSon's Land Services — B2B Intro Call Scripts

*One script per category. Keep calls under 2 minutes. Goal of the call: a name, permission to follow up, and (where it fits) an email address. The follow-up email drafts are saved in Gmail Drafts.*

---

## 1. Tree services — the referral pitch

"Hi, this is Chris Andres with ThorSon's Land Services here in Camden — do you have 60 seconds?

My father and I run a veteran-owned stump grinding crew. I know you guys do the tree work — I wanted to introduce us as the guys you can call when a job leaves stumps behind. We're insured, do free estimates, and turn things around fast so your customers aren't waiting.

Would it be alright if I sent you a quick follow-up email so you've got our number on file?"

*If yes: get the name + email, send Gmail draft "Stump grinding partner in Camden."*

## 2. Landscapers — the site-prep pitch

"Hi, this is Chris Andres with ThorSon's Land Services in Camden — got a minute?

My father and I run a veteran-owned land crew — stump grinding, forestry mulching, brush hogging. When you land on an overgrown lot or a yard full of stumps that's outside what your crew takes on, we'd love to prep the site so you can do the pretty work. Free estimates, insured, quick turnaround.

Can I grab your email to send our info over?"

*Follow-up: Gmail draft "Site prep help for your landscaping jobs."*

## 6. Municipalities / public works — the vendor-list pitch

*Follow-up: Gmail draft "Vendor introduction — ThorSon's Land Services." Note: expect a formal bid/vendor registration process, not a handshake deal.*

---

**General tips for all calls:**
- Call mid-morning (9–11) — crews are out, office staff answer.
- If you get voicemail: name, company, one line on what you do, number twice, hang up in 20 seconds.
- Write down the name of whoever you spoke with — use it in the follow-up email.
- Camden-local businesses: offer to stop by in person. Face time beats phone time.

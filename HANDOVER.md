## Brand & messaging rules (hard constraints — do not break these)

1. Always say **"estimate"**, never "quote" — "quote" implies locked-in pricing in this trade.
2. Never publish the home street address. This is a service-area business; the street address stays private.
3. "Insured" is approved for marketing. **"Licensed" is NOT confirmed — never claim it.**
4. Never invent: years in business, certifications, review counts, awards, or military branch/rank/years of service.
5. Never present stock or AI-generated photos as real company work.
6. Lead with **veteran-owned** — no local competitor does, and it's the strongest differentiator.
7. Job requirement: the landowner must call **811** before any work (free underground-utility marking, ~3 business days lead time). Work cannot begin until utilities are marked. This is on the website FAQ.
